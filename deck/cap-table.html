Notes and assumptions

  1. Incorporation. Vexen is operated by its founder and is not yet incorporated. The intention is a Delaware C corporation formed at or shortly before a first financing, with 10,000,000 shares of common stock authorised and issued to the founder.
  2. Vesting. Founder shares are intended to carry a standard four-year vesting schedule with a one-year cliff from the date of incorporation, so an investor is not funding fully vested founder equity.
  3. Intellectual property. All code, designs, documentation and content were created solely by the founder, with no contractors, agencies or co-authors, and no employer, university or third party has any claim to it. All IP will be assigned to the company on incorporation.
  4. Headcount. No employees or contractors. First two intended hires are a founding engineer for the credential broker and a first go-to-market hire, both to be compensated from an option pool established at the first priced round.
  5. Cash, burn and runway. Cash on hand $50,000, all founder-contributed. Monthly burn approximately $200, limited to hosting, domains and API usage, so runway is not a constraint. Pre-revenue by choice while enforcement guarantees are completed.
  6. Product status. Vexen is live in production with over 5,000 users, built solo over two months, with no paid acquisition and no outside capital.
